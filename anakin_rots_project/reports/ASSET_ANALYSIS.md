## 5. Hair-card geometry

  24–30 verts = 12–15 rows × 2 columns; wisps have 6–16).
* UV mapping is **ByVertice** (one UV per vertex — no UV seams inside a card). Cards occupy **one 1/8-wide column** of the
  planes atlas (444 cards, u-width 0.125) or a half column (19 cards, u-width 0.0625); column starts are at multiples of

## 6. Haircap
The cap is a closed scalp shell (two main islands: the main dome and a thin front/side-burn strip matching the two
islands of the cap texture). Against the supplied head mesh it sits 0–3.9 cm from the skin (mean 0.95 cm);
108 of 704 cap vertices are marginally inside the head surface (the head is the lod *.001 re-export, so sub-millimetre
interpenetration is expected and is hidden by the skin/scalp ordering in game).

## 7. Head mesh (`head.fbx`)
Not a Frosty export: Blender 5.2.2 FBX, one mesh `mat_head:heads_anakin_01_mesh_lod.001`, 4 145 verts / 8 049 tris,
one material, **no skeleton, no vertex groups**, local translation (+35.19 cm, 0, 0) and 100× scale baked by the
re-export. After zeroing that X offset the head lines up with the hair cap (cap hugs the scalp, nose clear of the fringe).
It is used **only as a collision/fit reference (`HEAD_REF`)**, never exported.

## 9. Observations vs. the Episode III hairstyle (drive the reconstruction)
1. **Part is on the wrong side.** In-game the part sits on Anakin's *right* and the heavy fringe lock falls over his
   *left* brow; in every Episode III reference the part is slightly left of centre and the front sweep crosses to his
   *right*. The whole asset is a near mirror-image of the film styling.
2. **Too long and too heavy below the ears.** Side cards reach the chin/neck (tips at z ≈ 1.63 with a 90th-percentile
   outward reach of ±12 cm) and the back ends in a blunt horizontal shelf at the nape (tips ≈ 1.595). The film cut ends
   around the jaw line at the sides and at the collar at the back, with layered, uneven ends.
3. **Crown** is acceptable but slightly inflated by the long crown cards (9.6 cm mean).
4. **Front**: fringe lock drops to eye level; film fringe reaches eyebrow level and is swept more to the side.
5. **Texture/shading** of the original is good: chestnut colour, strand breakup and alpha are all usable as-is.
